# TabStack privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

TabStack is a browser extension that keeps notes and to-do lists for your tabs and tab groups, with optional sync to Todoist. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What TabStack stores in your browser

- The notes and tasks you write.
- For each tab or tab group that has notes: the page title and address (URL), or the group's name and color, so the notes can be shown and reattached after a restart.
- Your settings, such as what to do with notes when a tab closes.
- If you sign in to Todoist: your Todoist access tokens, and your Todoist display name and email address, which are shown in the extension's settings.

This data is stored only in your browser profile (`chrome.storage`). TabStack doesn't read the content of the web pages you visit.

## What TabStack sends to Todoist

Only if you sign in to Todoist, TabStack sends to your own Todoist account:

- Your notes and tasks.
- Page titles and tab group names, which are used as task names.

Page addresses (URLs) are not sent. To sign in, TabStack registers itself with Todoist under the extension's name and redirect address. Data in Todoist is handled under [Todoist's privacy policy](https://doist.com/privacy).

## What TabStack does not do

- It doesn't send any data to the developer or to any service other than Todoist.
- It has no analytics, tracking, or ads.
- It doesn't sell or share your data, or use it for anything other than showing and syncing your notes.

## Deleting your data

- Use the delete buttons in the side panel to delete notes and tasks. Deleting them also deletes them from Todoist.
- Sign out in Settings to remove your Todoist tokens and account details from the browser. To revoke TabStack's access, remove it under **Settings > Integrations** in Todoist.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser. Data already in Todoist stays there until you delete it in Todoist.
